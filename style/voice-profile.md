## What this file should contain

- Your preferred sentence rhythm and length (short and declarative? longer with subordinate clauses?)
- Words and phrases you actively use vs. actively avoid
- Your stance on hedging, qualifications, and uncertainty
- How you handle trade-offs — do you name them explicitly? Do you take a position?
- Openings you like and openings you never use
- Your formatting preferences (tables vs. prose, bullets vs. sentences)
